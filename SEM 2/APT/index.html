UNIT I: PROBABILITY & STATISTICS

A. Basic Probability

Formula NameFormulaExplanation
Basic ProbabilityP(A) = n(E) / n(S)Favorable outcomes divided by total outcomes.
Complementary RuleP(A) + P(A') = 1Probability of event NOT happening = 1 − P(A).
Addition Rule (General)P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B) − P(A∩B)For any two events. Subtract overlap to avoid double counting.
Addition Rule (Mutually Exclusive)P(A∪B) = P(A) + P(B)When A and B cannot happen together, P(A∩B) = 0.
Conditional ProbabilityP(B|A) = P(A∩B) / P(A)Probability of B given A has already occurred.
Multiplication RuleP(A∩B) = P(A) · P(B|A)Probability of both A and B occurring together.
Independent EventsP(A∩B) = P(A) × P(B)When one event does not affect the other.
At Least One TrickP(at least 1) = 1 − P(none)Fastest shortcut for "at least one" questions.

B. Permutations & Combinations

Formula NameFormulaExplanation
Permutation (Arrangement)nPr = n! / (n−r)!Order matters. Arranging r items from n items.
Combination (Selection)nCr = n! / [r!(n−r)!]Order doesn't matter. Selecting r items from n items.
Circular Permutation(n − 1)! waysArranging n people around a circular table.

C. Statistics & Data

Formula NameFormulaExplanation
Mean (x̄)x̄ = Σx / NSum of all values divided by total count.
RangeRange = Max − MinDifference between largest and smallest values.
Coefficient of Range(Max − Min) / (Max + Min)Relative measure of spread, dimensionless.
Variance (σ²)σ² = Σ(xᵢ − μ)² / NAverage of squared deviations from the mean.
Standard Deviation (σ)σ = √[Σ(xᵢ − x̄)² / (n−1)]Square root of variance; measures data spread.
Coefficient of VariationCV = (σ / x̄) × 100% measure of relative variability.

D. ANOVA (Design of Experiments)

Formula NameFormulaExplanation
Correction Factor (CF)CF = T² / NT = Grand total of all observations; N = total count. Calculate this first.
Total Sum of Squares (SST)SST = Σx² − CFSquare every value, add them all, subtract CF.
Column Sum of Squares (SSC)SSC = (ΣC²/nc) − CFSum each column, square it, divide by column size, subtract CF.
Row Sum of Squares (SSR)SSR = (ΣR²/nr) − CFSame as SSC but applied to rows.
Error SS — One-Way ANOVASSE = SST − SSCUnexplained variation after removing column effect.
Error SS — Two-Way ANOVASSE = SST − SSC − SSRUnexplained variation after removing both row and column effects. Must be positive.

A. Painted Cubes (n × n × n)

Painted FacesFormulaExplanation
3 sides painted= 8Always 8 — these are the corner pieces of any cube.
2 sides painted= 12(n − 2)Edge pieces. 12 edges, each has (n−2) pieces.
1 side painted= 6(n − 2)²Face-center pieces. 6 faces, each has (n−2)² pieces.
0 sides painted= (n − 2)³Completely inside, not visible from outside.

B. Painted Cuboids (a × b × c)

Painted FacesFormulaExplanation
3 sides painted= 8Always 8 corner pieces.
2 sides painted= 4(a + b + c − 6)Pieces on edges (not corners). Summing all edges.
1 side painted= 2[(a−2)(b−2) + (b−2)(c−2) + (a−2)(c−2)]Face-center pieces on each of the 3 pairs of faces.
0 sides painted= (a−2)(b−2)(c−2)Hidden inner core, not touching any face.

A. Ranking & Ordering

Formula NameFormulaExplanation
Total Persons in a RowTotal = Rank(Left) + Rank(Right) − 1The person is counted once in each rank, so subtract 1.
Persons Between (Non-overlapping)Between = Total − (Sum of ranks from opposite sides)When the two people are on opposite ends of the row.

B. Venn Diagrams

Formula NameFormulaExplanation
2-Set Unionn(A∪B) = n(A) + n(B) − n(A∩B)Total in A or B, minus those counted twice (in both).
3-Set Unionn(A∪B∪C) = n(A)+n(B)+n(C) − n(A∩B) − n(B∩C) − n(C∩A) + n(A∩B∩C)Add all three, subtract pairwise overlaps, add back triple overlap.
Only A (exclusive)Only A = n(A) − (all intersections involving A)Elements in A but NOT in B or C.
3-Circle StrategyFill innermost → subtract outwardsAlways start from the center (A∩B∩C) and work outward to find "only A", "only B" regions.

UNIT V: ALGEBRA

A. Algebraic Identities

Identity NameFormulaUse / Tip
Square of Sum(x + y)² = x² + 2xy + y²Expand or factorise expressions of the form (a+b)².
Square of Difference(x − y)² = x² − 2xy + y²Same as above but subtract the middle term.
Difference of Squaresx² − y² = (x + y)(x − y)Used to factorise quickly. E.g., 99×101 = 100²−1.
Product Form(x + a)(x + b) = x² + (a+b)x + abExpand product of two binomials.

Sum of Cubesx³ + y³ = (x + y)(x² − xy + y²)Standard factorisation of sum of cubes.
Difference of Cubesx³ − y³ = (x − y)(x² + xy + y²)Standard factorisation of difference of cubes.
